## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? What constraints apply — the assignment, the deadline,
the team's experience, the rival team's testing on Thursday? Write enough that someone joining on
Wednesday understands the problem without asking.

## Decision

What we will do, in plain words. Present tense, concrete. If it names a library or a file path, say so.

## Consequences

What this buys us, and what it costs. Be honest about the downsides — an ADR with no downsides is an
ADR that has not been thought through. Mention what becomes harder, not only what becomes easier.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. One or two sentences each is enough.
